Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

NextWatch ("we", "us") helps you decide what to watch by learning your taste from the movies you rate. This page explains exactly what data we collect, why, and how you can have it removed.

What we collect

  • Account email. Used to create and secure your account, and to sign you in. We never share it with anyone else.
  • Ratings and taste history. The movies you rate, and the recommendations we compute from them. This is the data that makes NextWatch work — without it, we can't personalize anything.
  • Crash and usage analytics. We use Sentry (crash reporting) and PostHog (product analytics) to find bugs and understand how the app is used. Both are keyed to an internal account id only — we never send your email or name to either service.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, or any device permissions beyond what the app needs to run.

How we use it

Your ratings train a personal taste model that powers your recommendations. Your email is used only for account access (sign in, password recovery). Analytics data helps us fix bugs and prioritize what to build next. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately and permanently removed — your account record, ratings, and notifications — 30 days later. Logging back in during that 30 day window cancels the deletion and restores your account exactly as it was.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, either from Profile inside the app, or from a web page if you no longer have the app installed: Delete your account.
